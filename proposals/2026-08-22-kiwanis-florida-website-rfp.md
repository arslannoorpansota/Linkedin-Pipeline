## 3. Recommended platform

We recommend a **standalone WordPress site** as the primary approach, for three practical reasons:

- Your team already knows the WordPress editor, so day-to-day updates need no retraining.
- Membership, directory, and event registration are all well supported through mature, association-focused plugins, which keeps custom code (and long-term risk) low.
- ADA accessibility, security hardening, backups, and analytics are all achievable on this stack and straightforward to maintain.

Standalone means your own hosting, your own database, and full administrative control, with none of the constraints of the shared International environment. If during discovery we find your member and event needs are heavier than typical, we will flag a purpose-built alternative rather than force everything into plugins.
