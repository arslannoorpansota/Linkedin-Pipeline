## 1. What we understand you need

Your brief is clear on one point above all others, and we've built this quote around it: **this is a repositioning, not a visual refresh.** The site's foundation is sound; what has moved on is the proposition.

Specifically, you're asking for:

- **A wider audience** — founders, CEOs and growing businesses, rather than the current fintech-only framing.
- **A restated proposition** — helping clients identify what's really getting in the way of growth, create clarity, and turn that clarity into practical action.
- **Your voice on the page** — human, experienced, practical and conversational, matching the Epic brand emerging on LinkedIn. Explicitly not a conventional consulting or outsourcing website.
- **The conversion system retained** — clear CTAs, lead capture, tracking, SEO foundations, a sensible visitor journey.
- **The right hierarchy** — positioning leads, conversion supports. The site should not read as a lead-generation funnel.

We agree with the hierarchy point, and it changes how we've sequenced the work. Positioning and message architecture come first and everything downstream is built to serve them, rather than the reverse.

## 3. Our approach

**Phase 1 — Positioning and message architecture.** A working session with you, plus analysis of the LinkedIn content that's already resonating. Output is a positioning statement, an audience definition, a message hierarchy, and a proof plan — agreed with you before a single page is designed.

**Phase 2 — Information architecture and content plan.** The site currently runs as one long page with anchor links; the nav promises seven destinations that resolve to four real URLs. We'd move to roughly eight genuine pages, each with an outline signed off before writing begins.

**Phase 3 — Copywriting.** Every page written in your voice, derived from the LinkedIn material rather than from consulting-sector convention. This is the phase that carries your brief.

**Phase 4 — Design.** Brand expression and page design built on the agreed message, not applied over it.

**Phase 5 — Build, conversion and technical foundations.** WordPress build, CTAs and lead capture, GA4 event tracking, Search Console, structured data, metadata, redirects, and the performance remediation listed in §4.

**Phase 6 — Migration, QA, launch and handover.** Including a walkthrough so you can edit the site yourself.

> **One recommendation, offered because you asked for them.** Widening from fintech to founders and CEOs generally resolves a real inconsistency — right now the homepage speaks to fintech while every blog post speaks to small business owners, and a visitor notices within one click. Our only caution is that the narrower framing was easier to rank for and easier to be remembered by. There's a middle path worth twenty minutes of discussion: broad positioning on the site, with named sectors you've actually worked in used as proof rather than as the headline. Your call entirely, and we'll build whichever way you decide.
>
> **Second, smaller point.** Your name currently appears nowhere on your own website, and the About section speaks as an unattributed "I". Since you want the site to feel like you, a named byline and a real photograph are the cheapest, highest-impact change available. Worth booking a headshot session before we reach the design phase.

## 4. Scope of work

Included in the recommended option:

**Positioning and content**
- Positioning workshop and message architecture
- LinkedIn content theme analysis, mapped into site messaging
- Sitemap, information architecture, and page-by-page outlines
- Full copywriting for approximately 8 pages, in your voice
- Two rounds of revision per page

**Design and build**
- Design system aligned to the Epic brand (colour, type, spacing, components)
- Responsive page designs, desktop and mobile
- WordPress build on a lean, maintainable stack
- Editable page templates so you can add pages without us

**Conversion system**
- Primary and secondary CTAs mapped across the visitor journey
- Lead capture forms, reduced to a sensible field count
- Booking flow for the diagnostic call
- GA4 event tracking on every conversion point, plus Search Console setup
- Thank-you and follow-up paths

**SEO and AI-search foundations**
- Page titles, meta descriptions and heading structure
- Structured data: Organization with founder, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, Article
- `llms.txt` and an XML sitemap review
- Locale correction (the site currently declares US English)
- Redirect map for every changed URL

**Performance and technical remediation**
- Enable compression and browser caching
- Page caching and CDN
- Asset audit — removing unused scripts and styles
- Image optimisation pipeline
- Target: first-byte under 500ms and a substantially smaller page weight

**Launch**
- Content migration and blog template fix
- Cross-browser and mobile QA, accessibility pass
- Staged launch with backup and rollback
- Handover session and written documentation
- 30 days of post-launch support
