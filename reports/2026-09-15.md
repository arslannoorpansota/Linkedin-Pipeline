### 2026-09-15 — OPS — v12 account-first retired, v13 set, method documented

- **Agent:** Ops / process
- **Faizan's call: "this method is not working."** v12 account-first tested over two lists (33% then 11%) and retired.
- **THE FINDING THAT OUTRANKS EVERY FILTER:** across both methods, **every keep came from opening a website and seeing something broken or missing.** Sales Nav cannot see that. Filters only reduce how many dead profiles you open; they do not change conversion.
- **Keyword NOTs tested and BACKFIRED:** adding 8 NOTs to the lead search took results **30K → 37K**. Sales Nav keyword NOTs match profile TEXT, not titles. **Leave the keyword box empty.** Recorded in `sales-nav-winning-recipe.md` so a future session does not retry it.
